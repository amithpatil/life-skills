## Prevention of Sexual Harassment Assignment

#### Question 1: What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Unwelcome sexual comments, jokes or implied messaging.
- Unwelcome stares in a sexual way.
- Unwanted touching, hugging or invading physical space in a sexual way.
- Abusing positions of power to acquire sexual favours.

#### Question 2: What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

Report it immediately to POSH authority within the company or the grievance redressal boards.
