#### Question 5: What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

- Avoid alcohol.
- Avoid caffeine.
- Avoid naps during the day if you are struggling with sleep at night.
- Practice regularity. Go to bed at the same time and wake up at the same time everyday.
- Drop the ambient temperature of your bedroom to around 18 degree celsius to initiate and maintain sleep.
- If you are not able to fall asleep while in bed, you must move out of the bedroom so as to not associate the bedroom with staying awake.
