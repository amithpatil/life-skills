## Focus Management Assignment

#### Question 1: What is deep work

Focussing without distraction on cognitively demanding task.

#### Question 2: According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

- Schedule distractions.
- Develop a rhythmic deep work ritual
- Have a daily shutdown ritual.

#### Question 3: How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

- Schedule social media, family obligations, blowing off steam with coworkers and incoming help requests from co-workers to a specific time period of the day to avoid distractions during deep work.
- Create a plan to start implementing deep work. Start off with smaller time intervals or if the problem is small enough, to one problem per time. Focus on the problem at hand during these intervals and do not get distracted.
- At the end of the day, once you feel like you have hit the number of deep work intervals in a day or if you have made a reasonable dent in the problem at hand, list down all the pending tasks as an action plan for tomorrow and disconnect from work. So as to heal and restore yourself for tomorrow.

#### Question 4: What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

- Social media offers you shiny treats in exchange for minutes of your attention and bytes of your personal data that the companies then sell for money.
- They are an unsavoury source of entertainment. They are made as addictive as possible on purpose so that people stay hooked to them.
- Social media brings with them multiple, well documented, significant harms.
  - They fragment your attention leading to poorer professional performance due to a hampered ability to concentrate.
  - The more you use social media, the more likely you are to feel lonely or isolated.
  - Constant exposure to curated happiness can leave you feeling inadequate and depressed.
  - They correlate directly to various anxiety related disorders.
