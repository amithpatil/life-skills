## Learning Process Assignment

#### Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.
To be able to teach the concept that you are learning to someone.

#### Question 2: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?
How Dali and Edison used the diffused and focused modes:
When you are analysing a problem intensely and  you are stuck in a narrow field of thought, you have to allow yourself to relax and let your thoughts drift and allow your brain to bounce around ideas about the problem you want to solve. So that, when you do start focusing on the problem again, you can use all these new ideas to look at the problem in a new light.

#### Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
- Active Mode: Focused tightly on what you are thinking. It uses thoughts and ideas you are already familiar with.
- Diffused Mode: Loose thinking involving unrelated and new thoughts and ideas and rapidly cycling through them in an unfocused state.

#### Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?
- We get good at something with practice.
- Deconstruct the topic.
- Learn enough to self-correct.
- Remove barriers to practice.
- Practice for at least 20 hours.

#### Question 5: What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?
- Stay away from distractions (social media).
- Learn something new each day.
- Understand the new concept so well that you are able to explain the concept to yourself in clear concise statements without any ambiguity.
- Practice deliberately: Identify the gaps in your understanding and practice those specific concepts with a clear goal in mind and eliminate the gaps.
- Build something with a wide scope: to be able to tie all your concepts together, try to build something huge so that you get a top-down view on the knowledge that you possess.
