## Grit and Growth Assignment

#### Question 1: Paraphrase (summarise) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.
The speaker pinpoints one characteristic as a significant predictor of success, grit. Grit is passion and perseverance for very long term goals. Talent doesn't make you gritty. In fact data has shows that grit is often unrelated or even inversely related to measurements of talent. The best idea the speaker heard of for building grit is through growth mindset.

#### Question 2: Paraphrase (summarise) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.
Carol Dweck says that midnsets have a major influence on a person's ability to learn. And people who believe that they are fully in control of their abilities learn, grow and achieve more over time. They embrace effort and challenges, see mistakes as learning opportunities and appreciate feedback.

#### Question 3: What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?
Locus of control: The degree to which you believe, you have control over your life.
Having an internal locus of control is the key to staying motivated in life. You must feel that you have control over your life and you are responsible for the things that happen to you, if you want to feel motivated all of the time.

#### Question 4: How to build a Growth Mindset
- Believe in your ability to figure things out.
- Question your assumptions.
- Develop your own life curriculum.
- During difficult times, honour the struggle.

#### Question 5: What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?
- Recognise that talent can only take me so far.
- Come to terms with my deficits.
- Understand that effort and challenges will only make me better despite how they make me feel.
- Stop running away from challenges and hard things.
- Learn how to produce highly directional and clean effort for any task or problem in order to optimise learning and growth.
- Challenge myself daily with a new hard thing. Even if i am not able to complete it.
- Don't let myself assume i have learnt something till i am able to execute it and replicate it.
- Learn to recognise mistakes and learn how to solve them. Build a mental map of how to traverse different mistakes.
- Be consistent.
- Recognise when rest is truly needed in order to keep motivation and persistence at optimal levels.
