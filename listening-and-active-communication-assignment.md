## Listening and Assertive Communication Assignment

#### Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)
Steps/strategies for active listening:
- Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts.
- Try not to interrupt the other person. Let them finish and then respond.
- Use door-openers. This shows the other person that you are interested in what they have to say.
- Show that you are listening with body language.
- Take notes during important conversations.
- Paraphrase what others have said to make sure you both are on the same page.

#### Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)
- Eliminate internal and external distractions and give the speaker your undivided attention.
- Display genuine empathy towards the speaker and be non-judgemental towards the speaker's perspective.
- Observe the speaker's non-verbal cues, tone and posture to better understand the speaker's emotions.

#### Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?
Due to over exposure to social media which resulted in a highly shortened attention span, my thoughts constantly keep drifting away when my mind finds the speaker's thoughts uninteresting.

#### Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?
- Empathise with the speaker.
- Don't let my thoughts drift away.
- Make notes and summarise them to the speaker so that I and the speaker can be on the same page.

#### Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?
- When I feel that my needs are not as important as the other person's needs.
- When I feel that the other person will not acknowledge my needs inspite of me putting them forward.

#### Question 6: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?
When the feelings of not being seen or heard overpower my rational thoughts, especially when I am having a bad or tired day.

#### Question 7: When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?
When the person I care about doesn't magically understand what I want despite me not telling them anything about what I want and it gets to a point where I start assuming that the other person chooses to not see the distress I am in.

#### Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive? You can watch and analyse the videos, then think what would be a few steps you can apply in your own life?
- I am calmly and thoughtfully put forward my needs as and when they arise and try to strike a balance with the other person so that both our needs are heard and recognised.
- I can learn to not undervalue my own needs and the needs of the other person as well.
- I can learn to fix my own reactions to the other person's needs.
- I can learn to recognise that my assumptions of how a person's actions makes me feel might not actually be true.
